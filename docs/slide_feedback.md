# Slide Feedback for Agentify Pitch Deck

Doc da xong `description.md` va xem toan bo 9 slide trong `Agentify.pptx`, ket luan ngan la: deck hien tai dep, sach, de nhin, nhung chua du sac de di thi startup. No dang giong mot deck "gioi thieu san pham" hon la mot pitch deck de thuyet phuc ban giam khao hoac investor.

## Danh gia nhanh

- Diem tot: visual nhat quan, problem/solution kha ro, co market, competition, GTM.
- Diem yeu lon nhat: thieu bang chung, thieu tension, thieu cu dam cuoi. Nhieu slide noi dung dung nhung con chung chung, chua tan dung het cac so lieu va insight manh dang co trong `description.md`.

## Nhung gi chua on

- Slide 1: tagline on nhung con an toan. Chua co mot cau hook that manh theo huong ROI hoac outcome. Vi du thong diep manh hon co the la: "Replace 1-3 CS agents on Zalo/Facebook with one AI agent".
- Slide 2: problem on, nhung phan dau chua dam. Trong `description.md` co cac so rat manh: `100-300 messages/day`, `30-50% missed`, `10-20% order errors`, `8-18M VND/staff/month`. Slide hien chua day cac con so nay len thanh headline.
- Slide 3 va 5 bi trung y. `Solution` va `Service Overview` dang lap lai. Voi pitch deck ngan, day la lang phi mot slide.
- Slide 4 co market nhung cach trinh bay con generic. Trong `description.md` co `TAM/SAM/SOM` kha ro, nhung slide hien moi nhan TAM/SAM, chua noi beachhead ro va chua the hien vi sao Agentify co the thang tu mot ngach truoc.
- Slide 6 business model con qua mo. Trong mo ta co pricing tiers cu the (`990K`, `2.5M`, `5M`, `8M`), ARPU, gross margin, LTV/CAC. Deck hien chua dua cac con so do len.
- Slide 7 competition on ve format, nhung con claim-based. Chua co moat ro theo kieu: `native Zalo OA + KiotViet/Sapo integrations + Vietnamese agent workflows + agency distribution`.
- Slide 8 GTM moi co kenh, chua co logic tang truong: `10 khach dau tien lay the nao`, `CAC`, `agency multiplier`, `partner channel`.
- Slide 9 ket thuc dep nhung chua chot duoc ask hoac why now.

## Nhung phan con thieu cua mot pitch deck

- `Traction / validation`: day la phan thieu lon nhat. Du chua co revenue, van nen co it nhat mot trong cac thu sau:
- pilot users
- LOI / design partners
- prototype/demo chay duoc
- so cuoc phong van khach hang
- phan hoi tu shop hoac agency
- `Team / founder-market fit`: trong `description.md` co nhac "Founder market fit", nhung deck khong co slide team. Thi startup ma khong co "why you" la hut nang.
- `Why now`: insight nay rat tot trong mo ta: `Zalo OA dang mo API`, `KiotViet mo webhook`, la window of opportunity. Nen co 1 cum rat ro.
- `Ask / next milestone`: neu di thi, nen co "We are raising..." hoac neu chua raise thi "Looking for pilot merchants / agency partners / mentorship".
- `Product proof`: deck dang nhieu text hon demo. Voi san pham agent, nen co 1 slide workflow/demo screenshot "tin nhan -> AI hieu intent -> tao don trong KiotViet -> gui xac nhan".

## Muc do thu hut hien tai

- Ve hinh thuc: `7.5/10`
- Ve tinh pitch startup: `5.5/10`
- Ve kha nang thuyet phuc ban giam khao: chua du, vi ho se hoi ngay:
- Khach hang nao da xac nhan can cai nay?
- Tai sao ban lam duoc?
- Vi sao bay gio?
- Chung minh unit economics / pricing o dau?
- Dang o giai doan nao?

## De xuat cau truc 8-10 slide

1. Hook: Agentify + mot cau outcome rat manh.
2. Problem: pain + 3 so lon nhat.
3. Solution: AI agent khac chatbot o dau.
4. Demo / workflow: mot luong cu the tu inbox den order.
5. Market: beachhead + TAM/SAM/SOM.
6. Business model: pricing tiers + ARPU.
7. Competition + moat.
8. GTM: 10 khach dau, agency channel, CAC logic.
9. Traction / validation / roadmap 12 thang.
10. Team + ask.

## Neu uu tien sua nhanh

- Gop `Solution` va `Service Overview` thanh 1 slide.
- Them 1 slide `Traction / Validation` hoac it nhat `Prototype + pilot plan`.
- Them 1 slide `Team / Why us / Why now`.
- Nang `Business Model` tu mo ta chung sang so cu the.
